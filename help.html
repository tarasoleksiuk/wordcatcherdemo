Word 😀 Catcher
How to Play
🎯 Goal

Words fall from the top of the screen. Your job is to catch the correct one — move your character under it or click/tap directly on the right word to answer the question shown at the top.

Complete all questions in a topic with as few mistakes as possible!

❤️ Lives & Scoring

You start with 3 lives. You lose a life when:

— You choose the wrong word
— The correct word falls past your character without being caught

Every 5 correct answers in a row earns you +1 life and triggers a combo effect 🔥

Wrong answer? The correct word is highlighted in yellow in the sentence — take a moment to read it before the next question appears.

🔍 Why? Button

Made a mistake and don't understand why? Click 🔍 Why? — the game pauses and opens a Google search with the exact question, asking for a grammar explanation.

Enable the "auto" checkbox next to Why? to automatically open the explanation after every mistake.

Tip: After a wrong answer you have 3 seconds before the next question — enough time to click Why?
🎉 Winning & Retry

Answer every question correctly at least once to complete the topic.

If you answer a question incorrectly, it comes back after ~20 questions so you can try again.

When all questions are done — you see your full stats: time, mistakes, average answer speed, and how many times you clicked Why?

🤡 Secret: Clown Mode

Double-tap your character (😀) quickly — it transforms into a 🤡 that automatically moves under the correct word! A 🤡 indicator appears below the Why? button. To deactivate, double-tap the 🤡 indicator. Single taps do nothing. If you used Clown Mode, it will appear in your final stats.
