How to Play
🎯 Goal

Words fall from the top of the screen. Move your character under the correct word or tap it directly to answer the grammar question shown at the top. Complete all questions with as few mistakes as possible!

✅ How to Answer
📱 MobileTap directly on the correct word block
🖥️ Desktop — mouseMove cursor to position your character, then click to shoot
🖥️ Desktop — keyboardA S D — select word 1, 2, or 3
Correct answer? The word block bursts with a green particle effect 🎆 and the correct word is highlighted in yellow in the sentence.

❤️ Lives & Scoring

You start with 3 lives. You lose a life when you choose the wrong word or the correct word falls past your character.

Every 5 correct answers in a row earns +1 life and triggers a combo effect 🔥

Wrong answer? The correct word is shown in yellow for 3 seconds. Click 🔍 Why? during those 3 seconds for a Google explanation. Enable the "auto" checkbox next to Why? to open it automatically after every mistake.

🎉 Winning & Retry

Answer every question correctly at least once to complete the topic. Wrong answers come back after ~20 questions so you get another chance.

Final stats include: total time, mistakes, average answer time, Why? count, and whether you used Clown Mode 🤡 or Slow Mode 😴

😴 Slow Mode

Double-tap the ⏱ timer in the top bar to activate Slow Mode. Your character changes to 😴 and a 😴 indicator appears below the Why? button.

In this mode the game automatically pauses after each new question — once the word blocks have dropped below the 😴 indicator. Take as much time as you need to think, then unpause by tapping the sentence or pressing Space.

Double-tap the 😴 indicator to deactivate. Slow Mode and Clown Mode are mutually exclusive — activating one disables the other.

Tip: Great for beginners or when studying a new grammar topic for the first time.
🤡 Secret: Clown Mode

Double-tap your character (😀) quickly — it transforms into a 🤡 that automatically moves under the correct word just in time! A 🤡 indicator appears below the Why? button. To deactivate, double-tap the 🤡 indicator. If you used Clown Mode, it appears in your final stats.
